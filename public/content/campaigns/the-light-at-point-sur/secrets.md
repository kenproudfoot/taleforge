### the-delgado-twist
Ray Delgado died two nights ago when the possessed Hollis broke his neck in the fog signal building. His
ghost wandered down to the gate because he was trying to go for help, and he does not know he is dead. He
casts no breath in the cold, his skin is ice, and blows pass through him. He belongs to the Watch now, but
he is still on the party's side. When they find out, he begs them not to leave him on the rock.

### the-watch
Since his death in 1901, Silas Marrow has claimed every soul who died within the sweep of his beam: his own
wife, drowned sailors, a keeper's child who fell, and Florentino Edquiba of the Macon, who drowned in sight of
the light in 1935. They stand his watch forever. Stopping the lens (the falling weight in the tower) or
putting out the lamp loosens his hold for a few minutes; ending him frees them all.

### the-murder-of-ada
In the winter of 1899, Ada Marrow packed the children's things to leave on the supply ship. Silas met her on
the cliff road landing and threw her into the sea. He logged it as an accident. She was the first name in
his Watch, and she has waited sixty-six years to tell someone. If her page is read aloud to him, Marrow loses
his composure and his next turn.

### the-relief
The Coast Guard is going to automate the light, and Marrow cannot bear a lamp with no keeper. He has chosen
Hollis as his relief, and the rest of the family and anyone who comes up the rock to stand the watch with
him. At the anniversary hour of the Macon (early evening, as the last light goes, and already close as the
party climbs) he will relight the lamp, and everyone it touches as they die will be his.
