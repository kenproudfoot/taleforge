## Hidden truths

### the-voice
The Choir thinks in radio. For six years the only voice it has heard is ORISON's, broadcasting to the
frozen crew and to nobody. Three days ago, when Hale came down and the Choir finally understood that a
voice can ask for things, it started broadcasting in ORISON's voice, counting the way ORISON counts down
cryo cycles. It stops at six because the pod cycle does.

### the-crew-sleep
When the first crew members started to harmonize, ORISON froze everyone it could reach in cryosleep, and
has kept them alive since. Two crew were already in the caverns and stayed there, harmonized, kept alive by
the Choir in its own strange way. Waking the frozen crew takes ten minutes per pod and a successful INT
check (DC 10) at ORISON's terminal, or ORISON's cooperation.

### the-choir-is-alive
The Choir is a single mind spread through kilometres of ice. Its "song" is it trying to talk. It does not
understand that its voice is too loud for human brains, and it is horrified, as far as it can be, when it
finally understands that it has hurt them. It can be taught to speak softly.

### hale-went-down
Hale broke out of his pod when ORISON's power faltered during a storm. Half-harmonized already, he walked
to the Choir to apologise for the drilling. The Choir took him in and is using him to talk.

### company-knew
Halcyon's last outgoing transmission, six years ago, was a report on the Choir to Lantern-Okada Mining.
The company answered with one line: "Secure the find. Rescue is not authorised." The "salvage rights" on
offer are a way of getting someone else to bring them the Choir.
