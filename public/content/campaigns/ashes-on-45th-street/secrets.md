## Hidden truths

### gordon-paid
Gordon Hartwell hired Castellane's torch man, through Castellane, to burn the Hartwell Building. He meant
to destroy Evelyn's copies of the ledgers and make it look like she died in an accident at the office. He
did not know the watchman was there, and did not care.

### evelyn-alive
Evelyn caught Sparks setting the fire and got out down the fire escape with the real ledgers. She ran for
the docks to find a reporter who lives on a houseboat there. Castellane's dockhands, who had been told to
watch for her, grabbed her instead. Castellane decided she was worth more alive as leverage over Gordon,
and locked her in the Pier 9 warehouse. Gordon has since paid to have the warehouse burned at midnight,
and Castellane has not decided whether to let that happen.

### the-ledgers
Hartwell Holdings has bought nine run-down tenements in eighteen months, insured each for three times its
value, and seen each one burn. Fourteen people died in those fires. Every payment to Castellane's "fire
safety consultants" is in the books, signed by Gordon.

### dolan-threatened
Dolan's daughter Margaret is a clerk at City Hall. A councilman on Hartwell's payroll told Dolan that she
would lose her job, or worse, if the report said arson. Dolan has kept the Blue Lantern matchbook in his
desk drawer because he could not bring himself to throw it away.

### castellane-will-deal
Castellane rented his torch man out; he did not sign up for a murdered heiress and a front-page fire. If
Gordon goes down loudly, the ledgers name Castellane's outfit too. He would much rather Gordon vanished
quietly and the ledgers sat in his own safe. He will give the party Evelyn's freedom and Gordon himself for
that, and he will keep his word, which is the frightening part.
