# TaleForge core rules

A light d20 system built for a 30-60 minute session in any genre. The game engine applies these rules
and does all the arithmetic. The Dungeon Master (DM) decides *when* a rule applies and narrates the result.

## Stats

Every character has six stats, each a modifier from -1 to +3 (a +1 chosen at creation and a midpoint boost can raise one to +4):

- **STR** Strength: force, lifting, melee muscle.
- **DEX** Dexterity: agility, stealth, aim, reflexes.
- **CON** Constitution: toughness, stamina, resisting harm.
- **INT** Intellect: knowledge, logic, technology, investigation.
- **WIS** Wisdom: perception, intuition, willpower, medicine.
- **CHA** Charisma: persuasion, deception, presence, performance.

Each archetype is **proficient** in two stats.

## Checks

When the outcome is uncertain and failure would be interesting, the DM asks for a check:

**d20 + stat modifier (+2 if proficient) against a Difficulty Class (DC).**

| Difficulty | DC |
|---|---|
| Easy | 10 |
| Moderate | 13 |
| Hard | 16 |
| Heroic | 19 |

- **Advantage**: roll two d20 and keep the higher. **Disadvantage**: keep the lower. They cancel each other out.
- **Natural 20**: a critical success. It always succeeds, and something extra goes right.
- **Natural 1**: it always fails, and the DM adds a complication.
- A **save** is a check made to resist something (CON against poison, WIS against fear, DEX to dodge).

## Combat

- Turn order is narrative: the DM moves the spotlight around the table and lets enemies act in between.
- **Attack**: d20 + attack stat + 2 against the target's **Defense**. Party attacks are always proficient.
- **Defense** = 10 + DEX + armor bonus from the archetype.
- **Damage**: the weapon die (d4, d6, d8 or d10 by archetype) + the attack stat. A critical hit adds the die's maximum.
- **Hit points** = 10 + CON x 2 + archetype bonus.
- Enemies attack with the bonus and damage listed in their stat block. The engine rolls for them.

### Downed and out

- At 0 HP a character is **Downed**. They cannot act.
- An ally can **stabilize** them with a WIS check, DC 10. A stabilized character is safe but stays down until healed.
- Any healing brings a Downed or stabilized character back into the fight.
- A Downed character who is not stabilized or healed within about one round (one turn per party member, minimum two turns) is **Out of the fight**. So is a Downed character who takes more damage.
- Characters who are Out return at half HP when the next act begins. Nobody dies permanently unless the campaign enables permadeath.

## Signature ability

Each archetype has one signature ability, usable **once per act**. It comes back at the start of every act.

## Heroic Points

Each player starts with **1 Heroic Point**. After a failed roll, a player may spend one to either **reroll** or **add +5**.
The DM awards more (up to 3 held) for great roleplay, clever plans and big risks.

## Milestone boost

There is no leveling. When the party reaches the midpoint act, every character gains **+1 to one stat** of their choice.

## Campaign rules

A campaign may add rules of its own, such as a resource to track (Nerve in a noir mystery, Oxygen in space).
Campaign rules override these core rules where they differ.
